# My 90-Day DevOps & Cloud Engineering Learning Plan

**Name:** Sudiksha Sutar  
**Current Level:** MCA Graduate | Fresher | Beginner in DevOps Engineering

## 1. My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud Engineering

DevOps combines development and operations practices to build, test, deploy, and maintain applications efficiently through automation and collaboration.

Cloud Engineering involves designing, deploying, and managing applications and infrastructure using cloud platforms such as AWS.

## 2. Why I Am Starting This Journey

I am starting this 90-day challenge to develop practical DevOps and Cloud Engineering skills, strengthen my technical foundation, and prepare for entry-level DevOps opportunities.

I have basic exposure to AWS and cloud projects. Now, I want to gain hands-on experience with Linux, cloud infrastructure, automation, and deployment tools.

## 3. My Three Goals for the Next 90 Days

1. **Linux & Automation:** Develop strong practical knowledge of Linux administration, Bash scripting, and troubleshooting.
2. **Cloud & DevOps Tools:** Gain hands-on experience with AWS, Git, GitHub, Docker, CI/CD, and Terraform.
3. **Real-World Project:** Build and deploy a cloud-based application using DevOps practices, CI/CD automation, and basic monitoring.

## 4. Three Core Skills I Want to Build

- Linux Administration & Troubleshooting
- AWS Cloud Infrastructure & Automation
- Docker, CI/CD & Infrastructure as Code (Terraform)

## 5. My Daily Learning Schedule

I am committed to dedicating approximately **5 hours every day** to learning and practising DevOps.

- 1 Hour – Learning concepts and understanding theory.
- 2 Hours – Hands-on practical exercises and tool implementation.
- 1 Hour – Completing daily assignments and GitHub tasks.
- 30 Minutes – Revision and technical documentation.
- 30 Minutes – Interview preparation and troubleshooting practice.

**Weekly Commitment: Approximately 35 Hours.**

## 6. My Consistency Strategy

- Follow the 90DaysOfDevOps challenge systematically.
- Practise every concept instead of relying only on tutorials.
- Complete daily assignments and maintain GitHub contributions.
- Build practical projects alongside learning.
- Revise previous topics every week.
- Track my daily progress and work on improving weak areas.
- Maintain consistency without skipping practical exercises.

## 7. Where I Want to Reach

By the end of these 90 days, I aim to develop a strong practical foundation in Linux, AWS, and DevOps tools, complete hands-on projects, build a professional GitHub portfolio, and become confident in applying for **Fresher / Junior DevOps Engineer and Cloud Engineer roles**.

**My Commitment:** Consistency over perfection. Learn, Practise, Build, and Improve — Every Single Day.
